Why I built it

A few cards into the points hobby, I was standing in Whole Foods trying to work out whether my Prime Visa or my Amex Gold earned more there. Browser extensions can help when you shop online, but nothing helps at a register.

My first idea was a ghost card: one physical card that charges whichever of my cards is best. The card networks don't allow that, and Apple Pay is locked down. So I built the next best thing. Before I shop, I ask in plain words, and earnrate does the research.

I'm about to go to Trader Joe's. Which card should I use?

Questions

Do I have to enter my cards?

No. With no cards, every answer covers every card earnrate knows. Name your cards for one answer, or save them to track caps, picks and welcome bonuses.

Does it ever see my card numbers?

No. Cards are saved by product name: "Amex Gold". earnrate never asks for card numbers, expiry dates, security codes or bank logins, and won't save a name that looks like a card number.

Where does my wallet live?

On this site, in your browser's storage; clearing site data deletes it. In Claude, in an anonymous wallet on earnrate's server, encrypted so only your recovery code or your connected apps can open it. To use that one here too, choose "Use the same wallet as Claude" in My wallet. On the command line, in a file on your computer. The privacy page has the details.

How do you check the rates?

Every rule comes from the issuer's own pages and carries a short quote from them. A check reloads those pages automatically and flags any change, so the fine print is watched too.

Why don't some perks count?

earnrate compares what a card earns. Store cards only come up at their own stores, and airline and hotel perks like bags or free nights aren't counted.

How does earnrate make money?

It doesn't take referral fees or show ads. The code is free and open source; companies that want to use the card data commercially can license it.

Can an AI agent use it?

Yes. Answers come back with a stable card ID as well as the explanation, so an agent that pays for you can use them directly. It never needs or returns a card number.
