Support

Something wrong with an answer, a sign-in or your saved wallet? Here's how to fix the common problems and how to reach a person.

A rate looks wrong

Every answer names the rule it used and quotes the issuer's page behind it. If the issuer's page says something different, or a store is counted in the wrong category, tell us with the card, the store and a link to the issuer's page. Corrections come from the issuer's own terms, so that link is what lets us fix it.

Report it as a GitHub issue or by email at support@earnrate.dev.

Connecting earnrate to an AI app

When you add earnrate to an app like Claude, it opens earnrate's sign-in page once. Choose Start a wallet for a new, empty wallet, or Already have a wallet? and type your recovery code to connect one you already have. There's no email or password. If the app keeps asking you to sign in, remove earnrate from the app's connector settings and add it again.

Never type your recovery code into the chat. earnrate only asks for it on its own page.

Your saved wallet

  • Lost your recovery code? earnrate can't look it up or reset it, because the wallet isn't tied to an email. Apps that are already connected keep working. To connect a new app, start a new wallet.
  • Change your recovery code at mcp.earnrate.dev/change-code. The old code stops working and connected apps are signed out.
  • Use another wallet: ask the app to switch wallets, then connect on earnrate's page with that wallet's recovery code.
  • Delete your wallet: ask the app to delete your earnrate wallet. It's deleted at once. A wallet unused for 13 months is deleted automatically.

earnrate.dev and the command line keep their own wallets, separate from the one your AI app uses. You can move one between them by exporting and importing it.

Contact

Email support@earnrate.dev. Don't include card numbers or recovery codes: earnrate never needs them.
