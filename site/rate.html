Rate my wallet

What your cards earn in a year after their fees, next to one flat-rate card and the best two cards for the same spending.

How this is worked out

earnrate spends a year of monthly spending on the cards, each purchase going to whichever card earns the most on it, with spending caps filling and resetting as the issuer's terms say. Points count at earnrate's value for each program, or at the values set in My wallet. Annual fees come off the total, and statement credits count against them only once they're marked as used: until each is, the figure is a range. Lounge access and other perks don't count.

The flat-rate card is the best one with no annual fee that pays the same rate on every purchase. The best two cards are the pair, out of every card earnrate knows, that nets the most on the same spending.

A link to a report carries the cards, picks, memberships, which credits are used and any point values set, and the monthly spending if its maker chooses.
