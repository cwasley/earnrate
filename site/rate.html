Rate my wallet

What your cards earn in a year after their fees, next to one flat-rate card and the best two cards for the same spending.

How it's worked out

earnrate spends a year of your monthly amounts on your cards, each purchase going to whichever card earns the most on it, with spending caps filling and resetting as the issuer's terms say. Points count at earnrate's value for each program, or at the values you set in My wallet. Annual fees come off the total, and statement credits count against them only where My wallet marks them as used. Lounge access and other perks don't count.

The flat-rate card is the best one with no annual fee that pays the same rate on every purchase. The best two cards are the pair, out of every card earnrate knows, that nets the most on the same spending.

A link to a report carries your cards, picks, memberships, which credits you use and any point values you set, and your spending if you choose. Copy link saves that on earnrate.dev under a short random name, with nothing about who made it, until you delete it or 13 months after it was last opened. The longer link is saved nowhere: the report is in the address after the #, the part browsers don't send to a server. What a link holds.
