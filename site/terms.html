What earnrate is

earnrate tells you which credit card is likely to earn the most for a purchase, based on each card issuer's published terms. It's information, not financial advice. It doesn't apply for cards, make payments or move money, and it isn't affiliated with any card issuer or network.

Answers can be wrong. Issuers change their terms, a store may be coded differently than earnrate assumes, and your own account may have offers or limits earnrate doesn't know about. Check the issuer's terms before you rely on a rate, especially for a large purchase or before applying for a card. earnrate isn't responsible for rewards you expected and didn't get.

Saved wallets

Connecting earnrate in Claude starts an anonymous wallet on mcp.earnrate.dev; saving cards to it is optional. You're responsible for keeping your recovery code: earnrate can't look it up or reset it, and anyone with the code can open the wallet. A wallet is deleted when you delete it, after 13 months unused, or after a week if it's never used. Export it if you want a copy. The privacy page describes what a wallet holds and how it's protected.

Don't put card numbers, security codes or account logins anywhere in earnrate, including card names. earnrate is meant for adults.
